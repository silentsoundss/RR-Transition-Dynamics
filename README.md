# RR-Transition-Dynamics
This repository accompanies my honors thesis in economics completed at UCLA. The goal is to examine transition dynamics in the context of misallocation. I replicate and extend the following paper:

Restuccia, D. and Rogerson, R. (2008). Policy distortions and aggregate productivity with heterogeneous establishments. Review of Economic Dynamics, 11(4):707-720.

There are two notebooks included. "RR_rep_and_extension" replicates the original paper and includes my first attempt at an extension with an exogenous TFP path. The replication is what I report in the final draft, but the extension is only briefly mentioned as it is not actually capturing the transition dynamics. The second notebook is a dynamic extension that utilizes two assets. This approach is written about in detail in the paper. I also want to acknowledge the use of AI assistance in the writing of this code.

The final paper is also included in this repository.
